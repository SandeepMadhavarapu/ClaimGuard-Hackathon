Verify a receipt

Load a signed proof receipt or run receipt downloaded from ClaimGuard. This page checks it entirely in your browser: the hash chain over every step, the signature made by a key that never left the device that produced it, and the egress manifest of what was sent. Change one character and the signature check fails.

What a valid signature proves: the receipt was not altered after it was produced. It does not prove the producing device is honest; the published code, the tests and the leak gate cover that.
